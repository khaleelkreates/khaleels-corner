Ready? Open my workspace ↗
1

Open the door

  1. Tap the seller link in your welcome message. Chrome or Safari works well. If WhatsApp’s browser struggles with uploads, choose Open in browser.
  2. Copy your access code, then paste it into Seller or administrator access code. Keep the hyphens and avoid extra spaces.
  3. Tap Enter workspace. Your own business opens automatically.
See the phone screen
2

Give your business its voice

Scroll to Business details. Tap any field to change the name, category, description or public contacts.

  • WhatsApp: use international digits, such as 2348012345678, without +, spaces or the leading Nigerian 0.
  • Instagram: enter only your handle, such as your_business, without @ or the full link.
  • Email: optional. This is a public business contact, not a login requirement.
See an example business form
3

Freshen up your pictures

Under Business logo or Main flyer, tap Choose File or the photo picker. Select a picture from Photos, Gallery or Files, then wait for the upload confirmation.

Pictures resize automatically. Keep the originals on your phone. Existing flyers stay in place until replaced. Use clear images with readable text.

Upload pictures intended for public display. Even a photo attached to a draft has a public image link.

4

Stock your digital shelf

  1. Scroll to Catalogue items and tap ＋ Add an item.
  2. Enter the item or service name, description and an optional price.
  3. Choose Available or Currently unavailable.
  4. Add up to two photos per item.

You can list five items. They can be products, services or property listings. A blank price displays Ask for price.

To remove an item, tap Delete item, then publish the change. Marking it unavailable keeps it visible with an availability note.

5

Save, check, then shine

Scroll down to the action buttons. On a phone, they appear below the form.

Save draft
Keeps your work for later. Your visitors still see the previous published version.

Preview
Shows how your current catalogue entries look, including unsaved edits. Preview does not save your work.

Publish changes
Makes your saved submission public. Wait for the confirmation before leaving.

This editor needs the Save draft or Publish changes button. Don’t close the tab while a photo is uploading or a save is running.

6

Send people to your shop

  1. After publishing, tap View public catalogue ↗.
  2. Check your pictures, prices and contacts. Allow about 30 seconds for a recent change to refresh.
  3. Copy that catalogue address from your browser and share it on WhatsApp, Instagram or with a customer.

Share your public catalogue link, not your access code. Customers can browse and contact you without logging in.

7

If something acts stubborn

  • Lost your code? Message Khaleel through your known business contact for a replacement.
  • Asked to sign in again? Sessions expire after eight hours. Use your code again.
  • Newer edit exists? Copy your unsaved wording, reload the page and apply it to the newer version.
  • Photo won’t upload? Try a smaller image and wait for the result. Source images may be up to 20 MB; the system compresses them to 400 KB or less.
  • Photo allowance reached? Each shop has 24 stored-photo slots and 10 MB, allowing room for drafts and published versions. Removed photos may remain while a saved/public version still uses them. Contact Khaleel if you need help.
