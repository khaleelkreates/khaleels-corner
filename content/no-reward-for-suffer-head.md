We really can't stress this enough.
Sometimes, we confuse effort with value.
We think that because something was difficult, it must have been worthwhile. Or that doing something manually somehow makes the result more legitimate.
It doesn't.
If there's an opportunity for leverage, please use it.
There's no point wasting time and energy trying to prove a point.
There was a time I printed 200 blank pages because my oga wanted me to count exactly 200 papers and bring them to him. 😂
If a token can  get your laundry washed, dried, folded and ironed, and save you the stress of washing, starching, looking for where to dry clothes, worrying about rain, and spending half your Saturday doing laundry, do it if you can afford it.
If a calculator can do the maths, use it.
If AI can help you get something done faster, use it.
If automation can take a repetitive task off your head, please Automate the boring stuff and save your energy for the things that actually need you.
Imagine still being stuck on "calculators will destroy the brain" in 2026. 😂
Sometimes, we make things harder than they need to be and call it hard work. Meanwhile, someone else uses leverage, gets the same thing done in half the time, and moves on to the next thing. So who's actually better off?
The point is not to make life unnecessarily difficult just to prove that you can suffer.
Use your brain to figure out how to make life easier.
Leverage is not laziness.
There is no reward for suffer head. 😂
