City wey butterfly no dey😂,The odds are already against you.

You meet someone amazing on a random Tuesday, but they live on the other side of the city. Suddenly, what should have been a simple ice cream date requires military-level logistics.
One person lives in Lekki, the other in Ikeja. Between traffic, fuel costs, commuting time, work schedules, and the sheer unpredictability of Lagos roads, seeing each other starts to feel like planning an international trip.
During the week, everyone is busy trying to survive. On weekends, one person has an owambe, while the other just wants to rest and recover from the stress of the week.
Even spontaneous dates hardly exist. You can't just say, "I'm outside." In Lagos, "I'm on my way" can genuinely mean another three hours.
Over time, conversations become less frequent and plans get pushed back again and again. The excitement slowly wears off because it becomes difficult to create time for each other, no matter how genuine the interest is.
Finding love is already difficult. Finding it in Lagos hard pass quantum physics.
