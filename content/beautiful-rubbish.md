Life will throw things at you that you didn't bargain for.
You can plan everything properly, do your part, and still wake up one day to find out that Nigeria has happened. 😂

A plan falls apart.
An opportunity disappears.
Something you were counting on doesn't work out.

And yes, you're allowed to be upset about it.
But at some point, you have to stop staring at the rubbish and ask:
What can I still make from this?
The same way someone can look at discarded plastic and see a bag, you can look at a failed plan and see a new direction.
Not every setback will make sense immediately. Some things will simply be rubbish.

But even rubbish can become raw material.
You can lose money and learn something you couldn't have learned otherwise.
You can lose an opportunity and find another path.
You can watch a plan fall apart and realise it wasn't the only plan available to you.
You can't  control every setback.
But what you do with the pieces? That one is still up to you.

Sometimes, what looks like rubbish is simply raw material waiting for a different perspective. ❤️
