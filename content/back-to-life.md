No matter how far you go to feel alive, reality will still set in.

You can travel, party, disappear for a weekend, mute your notifications, spend money, sleep for 12 hours, surround yourself with people, or simply pretend everything is fine.
For a while, it works.
Then Monday comes.
The bills are still there. The unanswered message is still there. The decision you've been avoiding is still waiting. That thing you keep telling yourself you'll deal with "later" has somehow followed you into the new week.
No be say enjoyment bad.
Sometimes, you genuinely need to escape. Breathe. Laugh. Rest. Remember that life isn't just work and responsibilities.
But escape isn't the same as resolution.
Sometimes, addressing the elephant in the room is the escape you actually need.
So enjoy yourself. Take the trip. Go out. Sleep. Catch cruise. Do whatever keeps you sane.
Just don't use temporary relief to avoid permanent problems.
Reality will still set in.
And when it does, deal with what needs dealing with.
Back to life.
Back to reality.
Back to work. 😂
