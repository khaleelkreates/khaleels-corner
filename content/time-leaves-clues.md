You don't always notice time passing.
Sometimes, you only notice when something you used to do effortlessly suddenly requires a little more effort.
Earlier this year, I felt almost immortal. I could stand for hours, navigate heavy queues, and my body wouldn't even flinch.
Then last Friday, four hours on my feet and my legs decided to resign from their post. 😂

Suddenly, the endurance wasn't there anymore. Even my left knee, which I injured a few years ago and thought had healed, decided to remind me that it still exists.
And that's the funny thing about ageing. It rarely announces itself with one dramatic moment. Sometimes, it's just small things you suddenly can't do the way you used to.
You realise that the body you used to take for granted is also changing, even while you're busy becoming the person you're trying to become.
And maybe that's why continuous development isn't just about your career, money or skills. Your body needs attention too.

Pay attention to the small signs. Don't wait until your body sends an escalation email before you start taking care of it. Because if you keep overlooking the signs, you go see wonders. 😂

And wonders, they say, shall never ... 😂

You can't stop time, but you can keep adapting and taking better care of the version of yourself that time is creating.
