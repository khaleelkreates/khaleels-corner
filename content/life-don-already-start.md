A lot of us unconsciously turn life into a series of conditions. When I start earning ₦X, I’ll enjoy myself. When I buy a house, I’ll relax. When I get married, I’ll travel. When the business takes off, I’ll finally get that thing I want.

The problem is that there is rarely a final milestone. Humans are naturally insatiable. You reach one goal, enjoy it for a while, then your mind quietly moves the goalpost. The ₦500k you once prayed for becomes ₦1m. The apartment you wanted becomes a bigger apartment. The car you dreamed of becomes the next model.

There will always be another level to chase.

That doesn't mean you shouldn't be ambitious or plan for tomorrow. It simply means you shouldn't sacrifice all of today in the name of a tomorrow that keeps moving further away.

Buy yourself something nice sometimes. Sleep well. Take the trip. Eat the good food. Spend time with people you love. Enjoy the small wins while still working towards the bigger ones.Because one day, you'll look back and realize you were living the life you once prayed for, but you were too busy chasing the next one to notice.

You  still dey wait make everything align first?  Oya nau Yamski.
