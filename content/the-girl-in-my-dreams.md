So there’s this girl. 😊❤️
We barely talk. Maybe it’s shyness, maybe it’s timing, maybe I just haven’t figured out how to cross that invisible line between knowing someone and actually knowing them.
But in my dreams?
We talk....
A lot....
And somehow, the conversations feel so natural. All the awkwardness, hesitation, and overthinking that exists in real life just disappears when I’m asleep.
Lately, I’ve even found myself wanting to sleep a little longer. There’s always a chance she’ll show up and we’ll continue whatever conversation my subconscious decided to start.
Honestly, it’s a little ridiculous.
We barely talk in real life, but apparently, my brain has decided we have plenty to discuss in my sleep. 😂
Then morning comes, and I have to leave her there.
She fades back into wherever dreams keep people.
I wake up, handle my real life, and quietly hope that someday, those conversations won't have to happen only in my dreams.
Maybe that's the funny thing about dreams.
Some are sweet enough to keep you asleep.
Others make waking up a little more interesting.
For now, I have both.
A girl to dream about, and a life to build. ❤️🥺
