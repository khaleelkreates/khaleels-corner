"Happiness is free."
We've heard it for years, and it's a beautiful sentiment. But is anything truly free?
Dey wii me...
Every choice comes with a price. Economists call it opportunity cost; the value of what you give up when you choose one thing over another.
You're worried, but you choose to let it go and enjoy the moment. The happiness feels free, but the price was your decision to stop feeding the worry
They're sharing free food somewhere across town. You decide to go instead of sleeping. The food may be free, but you are paying your time, your energy, and your rest.
Someone says, "I just want to be happy," and turns to drugs or alcohol. The high may feel good for a while, but the drugs weren't free. Even if someone else paid for them, you could end up paying with your physical health, your mental health, your relationships, or even your future.
A student skips parties to prepare for an exam. The result may be worth it, but the price was immediate pleasure.
Nothing is completely free.
Not success.
Not peace.
Not happiness.
Everything has a price. The only question is: what are you paying with?
No be every payment dey involve money.
Sometimes you pay with time.
Sometimes with comfort.
Sometimes with discipline.
Sometimes with your health.

So before you say, "Happiness is free," ask yourself one question:
This happiness wey I dey pursue... wetin e dey cost me?
Because every smile has a price.
Just make sure you're paying with the right currency.
