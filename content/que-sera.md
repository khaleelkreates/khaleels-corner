"What will be, will be."

It's a comforting phrase, but sometimes it's the wrong mindset.
Life doesn't always wait for you. Some opportunities expire, and some losses are permanent.
Imagine you're walking through Computer Village in Ikeja. Someone reaches for your phone, and instead of holding on to it or calling for help, you simply shrug and say, "If it's meant to be mine, it will come back."
It sounds ridiculous, doesn't it?
No one watches their phone get stolen and expects fate to return it.
You act. You hold on to it. You shout. You run after the thief. You do everything you reasonably can to protect what's yours.

Yet, when it comes to opportunities, relationships, dreams, and even our own growth, we suddenly become passive. We convince ourselves that if something is truly meant for us, it will find its way back, even when we never made an effort to keep it.

No whine yourself,  
You like someone? Talk.
You want the job? Apply.
You have an idea? Start.
You want to apologise? Do it.
Not every opportunity is designed to wait until you're finally ready.
We hide behind sayings like, "If it's meant to be, it'll happen," because it makes us feel better about doing nothing.
Sometimes, what is meant to be still requires you to move your legs.
Take the shot. Make the call. Send the message. Submit the application.
If e work, beautiful.
If e no work, at least you won't spend years wondering, "What if?"
Because regret usually hurts longer than rejection.
