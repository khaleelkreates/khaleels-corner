Be like this love thing sweet sha. I left home very angry this morning when I realized I didn’t withdraw money yesterday and would have to buy cash. Moving angrily towards the bus stop, this keke saw me from afar and entered my street to carry me. When I entered, he teased me that I was going to pay extra because he rendered a special service. He’s a funny guy, cracked some jokes which I responded to, and that made him comfortable enough to ask if he could get 1 minute airtime on my phone. He called the number, and I helped him dial it and put the phone on speaker. Apparently, it was his babe, and I’m like, dayum, this guy sabi him babe number by heart sha. They talked, and he asked her to wait for him at a particular spot. We got to the spot, she joined him in front of the keke, and this guy legit kissed his babe on her cheeks. Omo, see PDA o! They were playing love and playing with my life because why would a keke rider be playing love on Bariga road where the commuters are not normal? We got to our destination safely, the guy thanked me, and the babe thanked me too. I smiled. Make I no lie, very cute couple. That was how I moved from leaving home angrily to smiling sheepishly.
